Welcome to Pimlico Fresh

Located in the heart of Pimlico, we serve fresh, homemade dishes and exceptional coffee. From hearty breakfasts to delightful pastries, every bite is made with care and quality ingredients.

Whether you're starting your day with our famous French Toast or enjoying an afternoon coffee with a slice of our homemade cake, Pimlico Fresh is your neighborhood spot for great food and warm hospitality.
